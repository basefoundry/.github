# Code of Conduct

Base Foundry is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free
community for everyone who participates in its repositories, discussions,
issues, and events.

Expected behavior includes:

- being respectful and constructive, especially when disagreeing;
- assuming good intent while asking for concrete evidence when it matters;
- keeping criticism focused on the work, not on people; and
- protecting private information and security-sensitive reports.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks,
sexualized conduct, threats, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and publishing
private or security-sensitive information without permission.

If a situation needs maintainer attention, use the private contact path in
`SECURITY.md` for security or privacy concerns. For other conduct concerns,
contact the maintainers through the repository's documented support path and
include only the context needed to investigate. Do not create a public issue
for a private conduct report.

Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, restrict participation, or
take other proportionate action when behavior violates this policy. Retaliation
against a good-faith report is not acceptable.

This policy applies to all Base Foundry repositories and public project spaces.
Repository-specific guidance may add detail but may not weaken these baseline
expectations.
